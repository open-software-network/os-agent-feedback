Epode helps companies understand every customer—known, anonymous, or present for only one interaction—through
the AI agent acting for them. The company installs Epode once. Customers do not create an Epode account, install
a plugin, or send product keys anywhere.

## The four-screen mental model

| Screen | The question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Customers** | What does this customer care about, and how do we know? |
| **Insights** | What are we learning across customers? |
| **Setup** | Is the company integration collecting and returning context correctly? |
| **Data controls** | What may be shared, remembered, personalized, or used for advertising? |

Questions, capabilities, relays, signals, and sessions support those four jobs. They are not the product's
organizing model.

## The loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask">
    On an eligible product response, Epode returns one relevant, company-branded request for missing customer
    context. The customer agent can answer from the current task, ask the customer, report no relevant context,
    or decline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn">
    The agent sends only bounded context through the company's own same-origin endpoint. Epode records its
    permission, provenance, confidence, allowed use, and expiry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Personalize">
    The company's server retrieves only active context allowed for this exact purpose and adapts the product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure">
    The company links a conversion, completion, engagement, dismissal, or abandonment to the exact context used.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Epode is identity-aware, never identity-inventing. The company supplies verified `accountRef` or `userRef`, a
product-owned `anonymousRef`, or no stable identity at all. Epode never discovers a person's identity from an IP,
fingerprint, prompt, or agent claim.
</Note>
